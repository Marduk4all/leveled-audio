# Code of Conduct

Leveled Audio is a small, friendly project. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background, identity or the language they write in (English or German).

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to audio or to open source.
- Give constructive, specific feedback about code and ideas, not about people.
- Assume good intentions and ask before you judge.
- Respect people's time, privacy and the recordings they share. Don't pass on shared audio without permission.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing other people's private information without their explicit permission.
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or ignoring a maintainer's request to stop.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to all project spaces: issues, discussions, pull requests, and anywhere you represent the project.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, and block people from the project who break these rules.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Marduk4all). Reports are handled confidentially.

This code of conduct is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
