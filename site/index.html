Use cases

🎙

Podcasts

  • Guests on different microphones or remote calls end up equally loud.
  • −16 LUFS by default, as most podcast platforms recommend.
  • Album mode: one shared gain for a whole season.
🎬

Video production

  • Dialogue and voice-over for YouTube, social media, documentaries and corporate video.
  • Presenters who move around or turn away stay at a steady level.
  • −14 LUFS for streaming, −23 LUFS for broadcast.
🎓

Lectures & webinars

  • Quiet and loud speakers are evened out.
  • Less room noise in lecture-hall and screen recordings.
📚

Audiobooks & narration

  • Many recording sessions, one consistent loudness.
  • Controlled peaks for any distribution format.
📻

Radio & broadcast

  • EBU R128 preset (−23 LUFS) with natural room tone.
  • JSON report with all measurements for QA and archives.

What it does

Adaptive leveler

Evens out speakers and phrases. Gain changes happen in pauses, and pauses are not boosted. Up to 130 % strength for large changes in microphone distance.

Noise reduction

Finds a noise profile automatically in the quietest pauses of each file, or learns it once from room tone.

Handling noise

Detects low-frequency knocks and microphone handling noise, lowers them and never boosts them.

Loudness & true peak

ITU-R BS.1770-4 / EBU R128 normalization per file or as an album, with an 8× oversampled true-peak limiter.

Compressor & pause gate

Gently catches loud laughs and shouts. An optional pause gate gives a tight "radio" sound.

Safe batch processing

Originals are never touched, there are no half-written files, and the same input always gives byte-identical output.

Get started

  1. Download the archive for your system and unpack it. On macOS, move Leveled Audio.app to Applications and open it once with right-click → Open.
  2. Add WAV files or folders, or drag them into the window.
  3. Pick a preset, then click Export. Results are saved next to the originals as name-leveled-<preset>.wav.

Prefer the command line? The same processing is available as leveled-audio:

leveled-audio process recordings/ -r -o done/                 # podcast, −16 LUFS
leveled-audio process dialogue.wav -o done/ --target-lufs -14  # YouTube video
leveled-audio process interview.wav -o done/ --preset ebu-r128 # broadcast
